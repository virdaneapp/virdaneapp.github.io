This Explicit Consent Notice applies to Virdane Feyz. The data controller is Savaş Onur Demirci. Contact: virdane.destek@gmail.com.

The two consents below are independent of each other and optional. You can use the app on your phone without giving consent. You can give or withdraw your consents at any time in Settings › Privacy.

1. Cloud backup and Saf

Scope: account number and session information; username, short ID and the code of the avatar you chose; dhikr, Feyz, streak, Streak Shield and badge information; for Saf, the Safs you start/join, your contributions, invitations, blocks and reports; notification token for Saf notifications (if you allowed notifications); your consent records.

This data may be considered special-category personal data that can be associated with religious belief.

Purpose: backing up your data and syncing it between devices, protecting your account, making the Saf feature and Saf notifications work.

Storage location and transfer: Supabase (European Union, Frankfurt); Saf notifications are delivered through Expo, Apple and Google notification services. This means a transfer abroad.

If you do not consent: no cloud account is created and this data is not sent to the server; the app works fully on your phone — only Saf cannot be used.

Withdrawal: Settings › Privacy › “Cloud backup and Saf”. When you withdraw, syncing stops; you can request deletion of your cloud data in the same section.

2. Analytics and error logs

Scope: daily usage summary (number of sessions, time spent in the app, screen visits and durations, the screen where you left, counts of infrequent actions, selected theme), daily dhikr counts, Saf summaries, Premium status, number of ads shown; device model, operating system and app version; location at country, province and district level (no coordinates are sent); app error logs.

Purpose: improving the app, understanding where users leave the app, detecting and fixing errors. This data is not used to build advertising profiles and is never sold.

Storage location and transfer: usage summaries in Supabase (EU, Frankfurt); error logs in the Sentry service. Converting location into an address is done by your phone’s operating system service (Apple or Google). This may mean a transfer abroad. Usage summaries are kept for up to 24 months.

This consent also requires the cloud backup and Saf consent. If you do not consent, no analytics are performed and no error logs are sent; the app’s other features are not affected.

Withdrawal: Settings › Privacy › “Analytics”. When you withdraw, no new data is collected and the analytics summaries already sent are deleted.
