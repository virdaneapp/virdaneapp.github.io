3. Saf and Community Guidelines

Saf lets you share a dhikr counter with people you invite by their short ID. In the free version a Saf can have up to 5 people, and with Premium up to 20 people (including the founder); in the free version you can start up to 3 Safs in 7 days, and up to 7 different people in total can join a Saf.

The following are prohibited in Saf titles, custom dhikr texts and usernames: profanity, insults, hate speech, obscene content, expressions that harass or provoke others, propaganda on behalf of a particular order, community or group, and sharing personal information.

You can report inappropriate content or behavior with “Report” from the Saf details or the member list, and block people with “Block Person”. A person you block cannot send you invitations and does not appear in your list in shared Safs; you can unblock them in Settings › Privacy › Blocked.

Reports are reviewed within a reasonable time. Content that violates the guidelines may be removed, the account’s access to Saf may be restricted, or the account may be closed. For urgent matters you can write to virdane.destek@gmail.com.
