This Privacy Policy applies to the Virdane Feyz app. The person responsible for your personal data (data controller) is Savaş Onur Demirci. Contact: virdane.destek@gmail.com.

Virdane Feyz works on your phone first. Your dhikr counts, streak and preferences are stored on your device first; internet or server problems do not prevent you from using the app. You decide, through the consents you give, which data leaves your device.

What stays only on your phone

The following information is not sent to our servers:

  • Your location coordinates (to calculate prayer times; if you do not allow location, Ankara city centre is used)
  • The custom dhikr you add, the selected dhikr, the counter and the goal
  • Your daily dhikr history for statistics (count and dhikr name per day; last 12 months)
  • Your theme, sound, haptics, ambient sound and notification preferences; prayer time reminders scheduled on your phone
  • The information shown in home screen widgets
  • The email draft in the account linking form

Cloud backup and Saf (with your explicit consent)

If you give explicit consent for cloud backup and Saf, the following information is kept on the server when you are online:

  • An account number created by the app that does not contain your name or email, and session information
  • Your username, the short ID shown as “ID” on your Profile, and the code of the avatar you chose from ready-made drawings (no photo is uploaded)
  • Your dhikr, Feyz, streak and Streak Shield counts, your badges, the days you recited dhikr in the last 21 days and your daily dhikr count
  • Saf (shared dhikr) information: the title, dhikr, goal and shared total of the Safs you start or join; your memberships and contributions; invitations you send or receive; people you block and reports you send
  • Your device’s notification token for Saf notifications (only if you allowed notifications)
  • Records of the consents you give or withdraw (type, version, date)

Accounts you may optionally link:

  • Email and password (your password is not stored on the phone; it is passed to the sign-in service)
  • Linking with Apple — the app does not ask you for your name or email; Apple sign-in is only used to verify your account
  • Linking with Google

If you do not give this consent, no cloud account is created and this information is not sent to the server; the app works fully on your phone, including dhikr, streak, prayer times and widgets — only Saf cannot be used.

Analytics and error logs (with your explicit consent)

If you also give explicit consent for analytics (cloud consent is also required), the following information is sent as a daily summary to help improve the app:

  • Daily usage summary: number of sessions, time spent in the app, visits and time per screen, the screen where you left the app, counts of infrequent actions (e.g. opening the Premium screen, purchase result, starting a Saf) and the app theme selected that day
  • Daily dhikr and shared dhikr counts, number of custom dhikr, Saf summaries (open Safs and highest member count), Premium status and plan type, number of ads shown
  • Device model, operating system and app version
  • Location: only at country, province and district level (no coordinates are sent; the address is found on the device by your phone’s operating system geocoding service — during this, the coordinates are passed to Apple or Google)
  • Error logs: technical error information, device model and version information when the app crashes or an unexpected error occurs

If you do not give analytics consent, this information is not collected and error logs are not sent.

Subscription and ads

Premium is purchased through the App Store or Google Play; your payment details are handled by the store and never reach us. Your subscription status is managed with the RevenueCat service; if you gave cloud consent, the subscription is matched with your app account number.

Users who are not Premium see non-personalised ads only at the bottom of the Saf list and The Path screens; there are no ads on the Dhikr screen. The ad service (Google AdMob) may process device and connection information (e.g. IP address, advertising ID) to show ads, limit their frequency and prevent abuse. In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, separate consent is requested for ads. The app does not track you across apps.

Where data is transferred

Your data is shared only with the following service providers, only to provide the service; it is never sold or shared for marketing:

  • Supabase (database and server service) — servers in the European Union (Germany / Frankfurt)
  • Expo (remote notification delivery) together with Apple and Google notification services — only for Saf notifications
  • Apple and Google — identity verification when you link an account; store payments; location geocoding
  • RevenueCat — management of subscription status (purchase records, app account number; your card details never reach us or RevenueCat)
  • Google AdMob — showing non-personalised ads to users who are not Premium
  • Sentry — error logs (only if you gave analytics consent)

Some of these service providers have servers outside Türkiye (the European Union and the United States). Transfers abroad are made with your explicit consent and with the safeguards required by law.

Special categories of data

Your use of dhikr and Saf may be considered special-category personal data that can be associated with religious belief. For this reason, this data is sent to the cloud only with your explicit consent.

Retention periods

Your cloud records are kept while your account is open and are deleted when you delete your account. Apart from the dhikr days of the last 21 days, no list of past days is kept. Analytics summaries are kept for up to 24 months. Report records are kept for up to 1 year after review. A notification record that you were removed from a Saf is kept for 30 days. These periods may be longer when required by a legal obligation.

The technical security logs of service providers are subject to their own retention periods.

If iCloud or Google backup is on for your phone, your local data may be included in the operating system’s backup; this depends on your phone’s settings.

Deleting your data

In the app: Profile › Settings › Account › “Delete Account and All Data”.
A Saf you started is handed over to its oldest member when you leave or your account is deleted; if there are no other members, it closes. Your contribution to a Saf remains as a number in its shared total and is not linked to you. Reports you sent, or that were made about you, may be kept separated from your identity for the retention period.
By email: you can write to virdane.destek@gmail.com, stating the short ID shown as “ID” on your Profile. This route does not delete the local records on your phone.
In Settings › Privacy you can withdraw your cloud consent and use “Delete my data in the cloud”. When you withdraw analytics consent, the analytics summaries already sent are deleted.
If you have a subscription, deleting your account does not cancel the store subscription; you need to cancel it separately in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

  • “Keep on this device”: your cloud account and all your cloud records (including Saf memberships, invitations, notification token, analytics summaries and consent records) are deleted; the dhikr, streak and custom dhikr on your phone remain.
  • “Also delete the data on this device”: in addition, the dhikr, Feyz, streak, daily dhikr history and custom dhikr records on your phone, the prayer times stored on the device (including location) and the notifications scheduled by the app are deleted. Theme, sound and notification preferences remain.

Your rights

You have the right to learn whether your personal data is processed, to request information, to request correction or deletion, to learn to whom it was transferred, to object, and to request compensation for any damage (Turkish Law No. 6698, art. 11).

You can send your requests to virdane.destek@gmail.com. Requests are answered free of charge within 30 days at the latest.

You can withdraw the consents you gave at any time in Settings › Privacy.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience. Users under 18 are expected to use the app with the knowledge and approval of a parent or guardian.

Changes

This policy may be updated. For significant changes, you will be informed in the app and, where necessary, your consent will be requested again.
